Warehouse & Storage Insurance

If other people's goods sit in your building, your exposure is bigger than the building — and it needs a coverage most property policies exclude.

Core coverages

  • Building & contents — the structure, racking, forklifts, and your own inventory
  • Warehouse legal liability — customers' goods in your care; the coverage standard property policies don't include
  • General liability — vendors, truckers, and visitors on site
  • Equipment breakdown — refrigeration, conveyors, and material-handling systems
  • Workers comp — warehouse work is a higher-hazard class; proper class codes matter

What drives your rate

What's stored (commodity type and value), square footage, sprinkler and fire protection status, building age, and security. Cold storage and high-value goods price differently than general merchandise — accurate commodity descriptions at quote time prevent ugly surprises at claim time.

Why agents pass — and we don't

Warehouse legal liability is the most commonly missed coverage we see on storage operations — owners discover the gap when a customer's goods burn and their property policy says “not yours.” We write warehouse programs that cover the goods, not just the walls.
