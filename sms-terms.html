SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This SMS program is operated by Nexus Growth Partners LLC, doing business as Nexus G Partners ("we," "us," or "our"). Program name: Nexus G Partners.

Program description

The program sends follow-up about an HVAC advertising audit you requested, and appointment or callback reminders. Nexus G Partners is an advertising agency for owner-run HVAC companies. These messages are not insurance quotes, and we do not sell insurance.

How you opt in

You can opt in in either of these ways:

  • Website form. Check the SMS consent box on a Nexus G Partners form and submit your mobile number. The box is unchecked by default. Checking it is optional. You can request an audit, and we can contact you by phone or email, without checking it.
  • Phone call. Give verbal consent during a call with our office and provide your mobile number for these messages. When that call is recorded, the recording is the consent record.

We do not enroll numbers from purchased lists. Consent is not a condition of purchase, and it is not required to receive an ad audit.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out

You can cancel the SMS program at any time. Text STOP to the number you received messages from. After you send the SMS message "STOP," we will send you a reply confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive messages from us. If you want to re-join, sign up as you did the first time and we will start sending messages to you again.

Help
