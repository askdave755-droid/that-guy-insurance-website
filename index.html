Insurance 101

Need a Little Insurance 101?

Insurance can be confusing, but it doesn't have to be. A few tips to get you started:

How much coverage?

Enough to protect your income, assets, and family. For life insurance, a common rule is 10x your annual income — but your situation may call for more.

Term vs. permanent life

Term is affordable coverage for a set period. Permanent lasts your whole life and builds value. We'll find the right fit for your budget.

Commercial auto basics

If a vehicle is used for business, a personal policy usually won't cover it. Commercial auto protects the vehicle, the driver, and the cargo.
