The problem

The job shows up on a phone. The ads usually don’t.

What owners are up against

A homeowner whose AC quits does not wait on a referral. They scroll Facebook and Instagram, then call whoever showed up. Owner-run shops are often on a job when that happens. Franchise brands have someone watching the ad account.

Shared leads pile on: one homeowner, several contractors, and the job goes to whoever calls back first from a list they all bought.

What we run instead

Nexus G Partners builds and manages your shop’s own Facebook and Instagram ads. The offer is a job you can book: AC repair, a system install, or a maintenance plan. When the season turns, the ads turn with it.

The lead comes back to your shop. We are not a lead marketplace, and the campaigns are not a franchise kit with the city name swapped in.

A Phoenix year

Summer demand, then the tune-up months.

The valley does not have a flat HVAC calendar. The ads should not either.

Summer AC demand

No-cool calls and emergency replacements take over the board once the heat settles in. Those campaigns need to be live before the first week the valley stays in triple digits, while the trucks can still take the work — including the systems a monsoon outage knocks out.

Tune-ups and maintenance plans

When the emergency pace eases, the shops that stay steady are selling tune-ups and maintenance plans. That is a different offer, and it needs its own ads. We change the campaign with the season instead of leaving a July ad running in November.
