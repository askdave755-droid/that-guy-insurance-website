Insurance can be confusing, but it doesn't have to be. A few tips to get you started: How much coverage? Enough to protect your income, assets, and family. For life insurance, a common rule is 10x your annual income — but your situation may call for more. Term vs. permanent life Term is affordable coverage for a set period.
