Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Operator: Nexus Growth Partners LLC, doing business as Nexus G Partners ("we," "us," or "our").

Nexus G Partners is an advertising agency. We run Meta ads — Facebook and Instagram — for owner-run HVAC companies. We do not sell insurance, and this website does not collect insurance applications.

This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you request an ad audit, book a call, or communicate with us by phone, email, text message (SMS), or this website.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us: name, mobile number, email address, shop name, city, state, and similar business details.
  • Audit details you choose to share: the jobs you want (such as AC repair, system installs, or maintenance plans), whether you already run ads, and notes you write on a form or tell us on a call.
  • SMS consent records: the phone number, the fact that the consent box was checked or was not, the time of a form submission, and — when you opt in by phone — a record of that verbal consent, including a recording or transcript when the call is recorded.

How we use your information

  • To respond to an ad-audit request or a call you asked for.
  • To prepare and provide the advertising services you request.
  • To send SMS messages you opted in to: follow-up about a requested HVAC ad audit, and appointment or callback reminders.
  • To keep records of consent and opt-out.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging (SMS) privacy

  • We send SMS only to numbers that gave express consent, either by checking the SMS box on a form or by giving verbal consent on a phone call with us.
  • The SMS box is unchecked by default. Checking it is optional and is not a condition of requesting an audit or of being contacted by phone or email.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • You may opt out at any time by texting STOP. Text HELP for assistance, or call (220) 208-0593.

See the SMS Terms for the program rules.

Mobile information sharing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We share information only with service providers that need it to respond to you, store a contact request, or deliver a message you opted in to — for example our SMS platform (Twilio) and our contact platform (Brevo) — and only so they can perform those services.

Data security and retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Consent records are kept as long as we need them to show that opt-in was collected, and as required for the messaging program. You may ask us to delete your information, subject to records we are required to keep.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of SMS at any time, using the contact details below.
