Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how David Edward Hughes, an independent insurance agent ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you request an insurance quote or communicate with us by phone, text message (SMS), or through our website.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address, and business details you provide on quote request forms or during fact-find calls.
  • SMS consent records: phone numbers, timestamps, and recordings/transcripts showing your opt-in consent for text messages.
  • Quote details: information about your business and insurance needs provided to generate quotes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare and deliver insurance quotes (life, commercial auto, and specialty trades).
  • To send SMS messages you opted in to: quote delivery links and appointment/callback reminders (typically 1-2 messages per quote request).
  • To respond to your calls, texts, and emails.

Text Messaging (SMS) Privacy

  • We only send SMS to numbers that provided explicit consent — verbally during a recorded call or via our web quote form.
  • We do not share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.
  • Message frequency varies (1-2 messages per quote request). Message and data rates may apply.
  • You may opt out at any time by texting STOP. Text HELP for assistance.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with insurance carriers and service providers as needed to prepare your quotes and deliver messages (for example, our SMS platform, Twilio), and only under confidentiality obligations.

Data Security & Retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Call recordings and consent records are retained as long as needed to demonstrate opt-in compliance. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.
